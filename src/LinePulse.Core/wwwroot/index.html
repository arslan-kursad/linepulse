Leak-test fixtures: early warning

Each fixture is compared with its own recent history and with the other fixtures testing the same pumps. A seal that is going off reads high on one fixture long before any part fails, so the warning arrives while every part still passes. Limit crossings are a late signal; drift is the early one.
